Approach

Three tiers. A free rules parser reads every document and checks its own arithmetic. A cheap LLM (mercury-3, 1 unit) cross-checks. An expensive LLM (gpt-oss-120b, 20 units) arbitrates documents the rules could not verify or where sources disagree. Fields are decided by majority vote, and a ledger keeps spend inside the 2,100-unit budget.
